About

A clinician's eye on insurance operations

Medical Claims Audit and Adjudication professional with 3.5+ years of hands-on experience across India's health insurer and TPA landscape, combining a medical degree (BHMS, University of Delhi) with a Licentiate in General Insurance (III) — a rare dual qualification that bridges clinical reasoning with insurance operations expertise.

Currently serving as Manager – Medical Claims Audit at Care Health Insurance Ltd, one of India's largest standalone health insurers, where I audit 1,200+ cashless and reimbursement claims monthly as a standing member of the Claims Rejection Committee (CRC). My work spans end-to-end claims auditing, Fraud Waste & Abuse (FWA) detection, medical necessity review, non-admissible charge identification, provider billing analysis, and hospital utilization trend monitoring across 10+ clinical specialties.

Prior to this, I served as Medical Officer – Claims at Safeway Insurance TPA Pvt. Ltd., handling pre-authorization and cashless hospitalization evaluations across multi-specialty cases for multiple insurer clients — giving me a comprehensive view of both the insurer and TPA sides of the health insurance ecosystem.
